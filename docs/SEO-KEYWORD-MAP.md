# SEO keyword map & content plan

## Page-level keyword map

Every indexable page, grouped by role. Primary keyword is what the page's
title, H1 and first paragraph should center on; supporting terms are
secondary phrases the copy should naturally cover. Intent flags whether the
searcher is informational (comparing/learning) or transactional (ready to
enquire) — it should match the page's call to action.

Rules: no article ships without genuine usefulness (no filler), a named
author, a review date, general-advice disclaimer, and no time-sensitive
thresholds stated without an authoritative source and last-reviewed date.

Every published article now also carries a `seoTitle` field in its
frontmatter (kept under ~60 characters, distinct from the longer editorial
`title` used as the on-page heading) — new articles should set one too, or
the page falls back to `{title} | Influx Financial`, which is too long for
most of the headline styles above.
